SoundPush streams audio between your phone and your computer (Windows, macOS or Linux) over Wi-Fi or USB.

What you can do:
• Listen to your computer on your phone: play music, videos or games from the computer through your headphones.
• Use your phone as a microphone: the computer sees it as a microphone in Zoom, Discord, Meet, OBS and any other app.
• Use your phone as a headset: both directions at once, for calls.
• Play your phone's audio on the computer: app audio on Android 10 and newer.

Built for everyday use:
• Pair once by scanning a QR code or typing a short code. Paired devices reconnect by themselves.
• Low latency with an adaptive buffer, or a more stable setting for weak Wi-Fi.
• Opus or lossless audio, noise suppression, microphone modes and gain.
• Stream to several devices at once.
• Controls in the notification and a Quick Settings tile.
• Works over USB when Wi-Fi is not available.

Private by design:
• Audio goes directly between your own devices and is encrypted end to end. There are no SoundPush servers.
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later). Every feature is free for everyone.

You need the free SoundPush desktop app on your computer: https://github.com/FlyToRakib/soundpush/releases
