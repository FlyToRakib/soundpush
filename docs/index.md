# SoundPush

**Use your phone as a speaker, microphone or headset for your computer.** SoundPush streams audio between your
computer (Windows, macOS, Linux) and your Android phone over Wi-Fi or USB. It is free and open source, with no
accounts, ads or tracking, and audio stays on your own devices, encrypted end to end.

## What you can do

- **Listen on your phone**: play the computer's audio through the phone and your headphones.
- **Phone as microphone**: use the phone's microphone in Zoom, Discord, Meet, OBS or any other app.
- **Headset**: both directions at once.
- **Phone audio on the computer**: send app audio from Android 10 or newer to the computer.

Start with the [user guide](user-guide.md). If something doesn't work, see
[troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md), or look up the code in the message on the
